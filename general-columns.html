Achiral HPLC Columns

Various achiral columns with superior resolution for reverse-phase and normal-phase HPLC applications.

CellaChrom™ Achiral Columns

Two Major Categories

CellaChrom™ is our achiral column brand name, offering a complete range of Normal Phase and Reverse Phase columns. We use high-quality filling material and unique packing technique to ensure world-level performance and high batch-to-batch reproducibility.

High Resolution

Superior separation performance in both reverse-phase and normal-phase applications. High plate number due to size/shape uniformity of filling material.

Excellent Reproducibility

High batch-to-batch reproducibility ensures consistent results across different column lots. Rigorous QC/QA verification for every column.

Broad pH Stability

CellaChrom reverse-phase columns offer broad pH stability (2–8) with high-purity silica base, ideal for demanding pharmaceutical applications.

Reverse Phase Columns — CellaChrom™

High-performance C18, C18A, C8, and C4 reversed-phase columns for broad polarity range coverage in HPLC separations.

Reverse Phase
CellaChrom-C18 & C18A

High-performance reversed-phase C18 columns with superior peak shape and broad pH stability. The workhorse for routine and demanding RP-HPLC. C18A offers an alternative ligand density for different selectivity. Suitable for polar to non-polar compounds across a wide range of molecular weights.

CellaChrom-C8

Medium-polarity reversed-phase column for polar analytes that are over-retained on C18. Ideal when shorter retention time is preferred. Excellent for basic compounds with better peak symmetry.

CellaChrom-C4

Short-chain reversed-phase column for large biomolecules including proteins, peptides, and oligonucleotides that require gentle chromatographic conditions with minimal hydrophobic interaction.

Normal Phase Columns — CellaChrom™

High-purity silica and aminopropyl columns for normal-phase HPLC separations, ideal for non-polar and moderately polar analytes.

Normal Phase
CellaChrom-Sil

Bare silica normal-phase column for separation of non-polar compounds, geometric isomers, and lipid classes. Suitable for use with hexane-based mobile phases. High-purity silica base ensures low baseline noise and consistent retention behaviour.

CellaChrom-APS (Aminopropyl Silica)

Aminopropyl functionalized silica column offering both normal-phase (with hexane) and weak anion-exchange (with aqueous phases) capability. Ideal for carbohydrates, nucleosides, nucleotides, and polar compounds. Also compatible with HILIC conditions.
