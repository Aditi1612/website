Who We Are

Cellachrom India

Cellachrom India is the official representative of Cellachrom Korea in India, the global chromatography brand of Cellion Biomed Inc., South Korea. We bring the world-class chromatography solutions trusted by researchers, pharmaceutical companies, and analytical laboratories worldwide from Cellachrom™.

As part of the Cellachrom global network, our mission is to make advanced high quality chiral and achiral HPLC columns, preparative columns, guard columns, and specialty chromatography reagents easily accessible to scientists and industries across India at affordable cost. By combining Korean innovation with a strong Indian service presence, Cellachrom India ensures that cutting-edge chromatography technology is now within your reach.

We are committed to provide:

  • Genuine and original Cellachrom products with full technical support and application guidance tailored for end users in India.
  • Cost-effective solutions without compromising performance.
  • Global quality standards with reliable local availability.

Our Products

HPLC Columns for Every Application

Our column portfolio covers the full range of chiral and achiral separation requirements — from polysaccharide-based chiral columns to preparative and guard columns.

AmyloSil™

Amylose-based polysaccharide CSP. Available as coated (A01, A02) and immobilized (B01, B02) types. Broad enantioselectivity across a wide range of compound classes.

  • Amylose tris-(3,5-dimethylphenylcarbamate) & tris-(3-chloro-4-methylphenylcarbamate)
  • Normal phase & reversed phase compatible
  • 5 µm and 3 µm particle sizes
View Specs →

CelluloSil®

Cellulose-based polysaccharide CSP (2024). Available as coated (A01, A02) and immobilized (B01, B02) types. Complementary selectivity to AmyloSil columns.

  • Cellulose tris-(3,5-dimethylphenylcarbamate) & tris-(3-chloro-4-methylphenylcarbamate)
  • Normal phase, reversed phase & SFC
  • Ideal second column in screening strategies
View Specs →

CrownSil®

Exclusive 18-crown-6 acid CSP with unique methyl functional group (2023). Specialized for amino acids, aminoalcohols, and amines separation. Only from CellaChrom.

  • Primary amines, amides, aminoalcohols
  • Reversed phase (acidic mobile phase)
  • (+) and (-) configurations available
View Specs →

Highest resolution among chiral columns • Long-term durability (even in 100% THF conditions) • Amino acid & peptide separation through CrownSil®

Reverse Phase — CellaChrom™

Superior resolution achiral columns for reverse-phase applications. CellaChrom-C18, C18A, C8, and C4 cover the full range of polarity requirements.

  • CellaChrom-C18 / C18A / C8 / C4
  • Broad pH stability, high peak symmetry
  • Consistent batch-to-batch reproducibility
View Specs →

Normal Phase — CellaChrom™

High-performance normal-phase silica columns for non-polar and moderately polar analytes. CellaChrom-Sil and CellaChrom-APS for broad applicability.

  • CellaChrom-Sil & CellaChrom-APS
  • High-purity silica base
  • Various particle sizes and dimensions
View Specs →

Preparative Chiral Columns

Large-scale chiral preparative columns for purification and isolation of enantiomers. Available in AmyloSil™, CelluloSil®, and CrownSil® stationary phases for scale-up from analytical work.

  • Scale-up from analytical to preparative
  • All chiral CSP phases available in prep format
  • SFC-compatible preparative columns

SFC Preparative Columns

Supercritical Fluid Chromatography (SFC) preparative columns for high-throughput, solvent-efficient chiral purification. Optimized for CO2-based mobile phases.

  • SFC-optimized packing and hardware
  • 3 µm particles for high efficiency
  • Ideal for pharmaceutical chiral purification

Chiral Guard Columns

Matched guard columns for AmyloSil™, CelluloSil®, and CrownSil® analytical columns. Same stationary phase — zero impact on selectivity. Attach before the main column to protect it from contamination.

  • 4.6 × 10 mm standard dimensions
  • Cartridge-style easy replacement
  • Available for all chiral lines

Achiral Guard Columns

Guard columns for CellaChrom™ achiral analytical columns. Protect your C18, C8, C4, Sil, and APS columns and maintain peak shape across thousands of injections.

  • Matched C18, C8, C4, APS, Sil phases
  • Quick-change cartridge system
  • Extend analytical column lifetime significantly

Our History

25 Years of Chromatography Innovation

From RStech Corporation to Cellionbiomed Inc. — a legacy of continuous development in separation science.

2000

RStech Corporation Founded

Dr. SJ. Kim established RStech Corporation, laying the foundation for Korean HPLC column excellence.

2001

OptimaPak — 1st Generation

First commercialisation of the OptimaPak brand, the 1st generation HPLC column platform.

2002

ChiroSil

Launched ChiroSil — crown-ether based chiral column, the first proprietary chiral stationary phase.

2008

Hector — 2nd Generation

Second generation platform with enhanced performance and broader application range.

2018

Cellionbiomed Inc. Founded

Dr. SJ. Kim founded Cellionbiomed Inc. to advance the next generation of chromatography solutions.

2022

CellaChrom® — 3rd Generation

Launch of CellaChrom® polysaccharide-based chiral columns with superior enantioselectivity.

2023

CrownSil®

Advanced crown-ether based chiral column with unique methyl functional group — exclusive to CellaChrom.

2024

CelluloSil® & AmyloSil®

CelluloSil® (cellulose-based) and AmyloSil® (amylose-based) polysaccharide CSP portfolio. India operations launched.
