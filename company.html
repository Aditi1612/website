About Cellachrom India

The official CellaChrom™ representative in India — bringing Korean innovation to Indian scientists.

Who We Are

Cellachrom India

Cellachrom™ is a brand of Cellion Biomed Co., Ltd., Korea for its Column Business. Cellachrom India supports the marketing of Cellachrom brand products available throughout India.

Cellachrom India is the official representative of Cellachrom Korea in India, the global chromatography brand of Cellion Biomed Inc., South Korea. We bring the world-class chromatography solutions trusted by researchers, pharmaceutical companies, and analytical laboratories worldwide from Cellachrom™.

We work in close collaboration with Cellachrom Korea to deliver the same global quality standards in chromatography columns established at its headquarters in Daejeon, South Korea. Through this partnership, Indian customers gain direct access to advanced HPLC technologies developed by one of Asia's leading innovators in chromatography.

As part of the Cellachrom global network, our mission is to make advanced high quality chiral and achiral HPLC columns, preparative columns, guard columns, and specialty chromatography reagents easily accessible to scientists and industries across India at affordable cost. By combining Korean innovation with a strong Indian service presence, Cellachrom India ensures that cutting-edge chromatography technology is now within your reach.

Our History

25 Years of Chromatography Innovation

Our chromatography technology has 25 years of experience — from Korea to the world.

2000

RStech Corporation Founded

Dr. SJ. Kim founded RStech Corporation, establishing the foundation for Korean HPLC column manufacturing excellence.

2001

OptimaPak — 1st Generation Brand

First commercialisation for general HPLC columns. Launched OptimaPak, the 1st generation brand of HPLC columns.

2002

ChiroSil

Launched ChiroSil — crown-ether based chiral column, our first proprietary chiral stationary phase technology.

2008

Hector — 2nd Generation Brand

Launched Hector, the 2nd generation brand of HPLC columns, with enhanced performance and broader application range.

2018

Cellionbiomed Inc. Founded

Dr. SJ. Kim founded Cellionbiomed Inc. to advance the next generation of chromatography solutions under the CellaChrom brand.

2022

CellaChrom® — 3rd Generation Brand

Launched CellaChrom®, the 3rd generation brand of HPLC columns with polysaccharide-based chiral columns offering superior enantioselectivity.

2023

CrownSil®

Launched CrownSil® — advanced Crown-ether based chiral column with a unique methyl functional group. Exclusive to CellaChrom.

2024

CelluloSil® & AmyloSil®

Launched polysaccharide base chiral columns: CelluloSil® (cellulose-based) and AmyloSil® (amylose-based). India operations launched.

Leadership

Director's Message

Dr. SJ. Kim

Founder & CEO, Cellionbiomed Inc.

Daejeon, South Korea

"Our mission has always been to advance the science of chromatography by delivering products of the highest quality, backed by deep scientific understanding and a genuine commitment to our customers' success."

With over 25 years in column chromatography — from the founding of RStech Corporation in 2000 to the launch of CellaChrom® in 2022 — our journey has been driven by a singular focus: to create the best chiral and achiral HPLC columns in the world.

Our CrownSil® technology, featuring a unique methyl functional group on the 18-crown-6 acid chiral selector, is available exclusively from CellaChrom and represents a truly unique innovation in the field. Similarly, our AmyloSil® and CelluloSil® polysaccharide-based columns have been developed through meticulous research to provide the highest resolution and long-term durability.

The launch of Cellachrom India reflects our commitment to making world-class chromatography technology accessible to the growing scientific community in India. We are proud to partner with Indian researchers, pharmaceutical companies, and analytical laboratories to support their most important work.

We are committed to provide genuine Cellachrom products, full technical support, and cost-effective solutions without compromising on performance or quality.
