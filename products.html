Why Choose Cellachrom

Product Quality Commitments

Drop-in Compatibility

CelluloSil®, AmyloSil™, and CellaChrom™ columns are functionally equivalent to corresponding Daicel and Phenomenex products. USP-coded for pharmacopoeial applications.

Rigorous QC / QA

Every column is QC-verified for plate count, asymmetry, and resolution before dispatch. Performance certificates available for regulated environments.

1-Month Free Exchange

Columns that do not meet your performance expectations within one month are exchanged at no cost — our commitment to product reliability.

Exclusive Technology

CrownSil® with N-methyl linker modification and our immobilised polysaccharide CSPs are CellaChrom-exclusive technologies, not available from any other manufacturer.

Local Technical Support

Cellachrom India provides in-country screening services, method development, and column selection guidance — protecting your IP without sending samples abroad.

Column Rental Available

Evaluate columns before purchasing through our column rental programme. Particularly useful for pharmaceutical R&D labs screening multiple stationary phases simultaneously.
