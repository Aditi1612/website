Technical Support

Expert chromatography guidance from our application scientists — from method development to impurity isolation.

01

Method Development

Our application scientists provide expert guidance on column selection, mobile phase composition, gradient programming, temperature optimisation, and flow rate for your specific application, whether HPLC or SFC. We help you develop robust, validated methods efficiently.

  1. Compound Class AssessmentDetermine the functional groups, polarity, and molecular weight of your target compound to narrow down the best column type.

  2. Column SelectionChoose from AmyloSil™, CelluloSil®, CrownSil® (chiral) or CellaChrom™ C18/C8/Sil (achiral) based on your compound requirements.

  3. Mobile Phase ScreeningScreen normal phase (hexane/IPA) or reverse phase (MeOH/water, MeCN/water) conditions. For CrownSil®: use acidic aqueous mobile phase (HClO₄ or TFA, pH 1–2).

  4. Gradient OptimisationOptimise gradient programme, flow rate (typically 1 mL/min), column temperature, and injection volume for best resolution and throughput.

  5. Method Transfer & Scale-upTransfer analytical method to preparative scale for purification and isolation as required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What mobile phases are compatible with polysaccharide-based chiral columns?

Coated AmyloSil™ and CelluloSil® columns (A-series) are compatible with hexane/isopropanol, hexane/ethanol (normal phase), and methanol/water, acetonitrile/water (reversed phase) systems. Avoid chlorinated solvents and THF in high concentrations. Immobilised columns (B-series) are compatible with all solvents including THF and DCM.

What mobile phase should I use with CrownSil® crown acid columns?

CrownSil® columns require an acidic aqueous mobile phase — typically perchloric acid (HClO₄) or trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) at pH 1–2. Operated exclusively in reversed-phase mode. Organic modifiers (MeCN or MeOH) can be used to adjust retention.

How do I store chiral columns when not in use?

Polysaccharide-based columns (AmyloSil™, CelluloSil®): flush with pure isopropanol and store capped at room temperature away from UV light. For CrownSil®: flush with water/methanol (50:50) and store capped. Never let columns dry out or freeze.

Can CellaChrom columns be used with SFC systems?

Yes. AmyloSil™-A01 and CelluloSil®-A01 in 3 µm particle size are specifically recommended for SFC applications. Standard 5 µm columns can also be used for SFC with appropriate system back-pressure (100 bar BPR). Contact us for SFC method development guidance.

Are Cellachrom polysaccharide columns compatible with Daicel conditions?

Yes. AmyloSil™ and CelluloSil® amylose and cellulose-based columns are compatible with Daicel column conditions. You can typically use the same mobile phase conditions as CHIRALCEL or CHIRALPAK columns from Daicel as starting points.

What is the maximum operating pressure for Cellachrom columns?

Cellachrom analytical columns are rated for a maximum operating pressure of 300 bar (4350 psi) for 5 µm columns and 400 bar (5800 psi) for 3 µm columns. Always operate within the specified pressure range to maintain column packing integrity.
